# Project: Vinniai/skills

## Design Context

### Users
Engineers and Claude Code users reading at a desk to understand the `abcdefg`
feature-development workflow. They skim to grasp the seven phases, their order, and the
Finalize gate — then get back to work. Reference material, not marketing.

### Brand Personality
Precise, disciplined, calm. Quiet confidence; the structure itself is the message.
Nothing flashy or loud.

### Aesthetic Direction
Refined Swiss / editorial. Grid-driven, generous whitespace, strong typographic
hierarchy, thin hairline rules, one restrained accent. Light "paper" theme (chosen to
fit an editorial reference read at a desk). Numbered, magazine-like treatment of the
seven phases. Anti-references: AI-slop tells — gradient text, side-stripe accent cards,
glassmorphism, drop-shadowed rounded cards, neon-on-dark.

### Design Principles
1. **Typography and whitespace do the work** — hierarchy through scale and space, not
   decoration or boxes.
2. **The grid is visible** — left-aligned, asymmetric, phase numbers anchoring each row;
   break it only for deliberate emphasis.
3. **One accent, used rarely** — ~10% visual weight; neutrals tinted toward the accent
   hue (OKLCH) for cohesion. No pure black/white.
4. **Editorial phase treatment** — phases as a numbered sequence separated by full
   hairline rules, never as repeated shadowed cards or side-stripes.
5. **Calm motion** — at most one restrained, staggered load reveal; respect
   `prefers-reduced-motion`.
